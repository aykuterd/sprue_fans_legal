Privacy Policy

Sprue Fans mobile app · Effective 17 October 2026

In short. We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, the kits, wishlist and ratings you save, and your subscription status. There are no ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and all data inside the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Aykut Erdoğdu, an individual developer, operating "Sprue Fans" (the "app"). Contact for anything in this policy: aykuterd@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

Stored only on your device

  • Paint rack (the paints you own) and app preferences are kept on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Camera: used only to scan kit barcodes. Scanning happens on the device; no photo or video is stored or uploaded. You can deny the permission and search by typing instead.
  • PDF reports you create are generated on your device. Only you decide whether to share the file.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party analytics or crash-tracking SDKs, no profiling, no automated decisions with legal effect.
  • We do not sell or "share" personal data for cross-context advertising, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

We may disclose data if the law requires it (for example a valid court or authority request). We do not share data for any other purpose.

4. International transfers

Our database is hosted in the EU. Subscription data is processed by RevenueCat in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA, the UK or Türkiye, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy framework, and for Türkiye the mechanisms in Article 9 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK). Ask us if you want details.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, lists, ratings and subscription records: until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your account and sign-in record, collection, wishlist, ratings, entitlement and usage records are removed from our database, and your customer record is deleted at RevenueCat. Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider expire on their normal rotation. Apple and Google keep payment records under their own legal obligations (for example tax law); we have no access to or control over those.
  • Server logs kept by providers are short-lived and rotate automatically.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR / UK GDPR, Turkish KVKK Art. 11, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) you can ask to: access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, and learn who received your data. Most of this you can do yourself in the app (edit or remove your items, Profile or Settings → Delete account). For anything else write to aykuterd@gmail.com from your account email; we answer within 30 days. See also account deletion.

You may complain to your data protection authority. In Türkiye this is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK, kvkk.gov.tr); in the EU, the authority of your country of residence.

7. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Access to every user table is restricted by row-level security so each user can only read and change their own rows. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting you occurs we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; for material changes we will also notify you in the app.

10. Contact

5. Saklama süresi

  • Hesap, listeler, puanlar ve abonelik kayıtları: hesabınızı silene kadar.

Barındırma sağlayıcımızın şifreli yedekleri olağan döngüsüyle sona erer. Apple ve Google ödeme kayıtlarını kendi yasal yükümlülükleri (örneğin vergi mevzuatı) gereği tutar; bunlara erişimimiz ve üzerlerinde denetimimiz yoktur.

Hiçbir sistem tamamen güvenli değildir; sizi etkileyen bir ihlal olursa mevzuatın gerektirdiği şekilde size ve yetkili makamlara bildirim yaparız.
